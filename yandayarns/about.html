About Yanda Yarns
Crafting quality, comfort, and style for every story.

Yanda Yarns is a proudly South African brand dedicated to bringing you unique, hand-crafted clothing and accessories. Our mission is to blend tradition with modern design, offering products that are as meaningful as they are beautiful.

Our Story

Founded in 2025, Yanda Yarns began as a small passion project by Ayanda Fimbisayi. The business has grown into a community-driven enterprise. We believe in ethical sourcing, sustainable practices, and supporting local artisans. Every piece is made with care, creativity, and a touch of young originality.

Our Values

Quality
We use only the finest materials and craftsmanship in every product.
Sustainability
Our processes are eco-friendly and we prioritize ethical sourcing.
Community
We support local artisans and give back to our community.
Creativity
Every design is inspired by culture, nature, and innovation.
